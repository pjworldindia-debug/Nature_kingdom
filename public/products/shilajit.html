The Conqueror of Mountains

Forged in the Highest Altitudes

High in the pristine Himalayan mountains, at elevations over 16,000 feet, a rare biological phenomenon occurs. Over centuries, plant matter is compressed by the immense weight of the mountains, slowly transforming into a nutrient-dense, mineral-rich resin.

We partner exclusively with indigenous mountain communities who brave treacherous altitudes during the brief summer months to hand-harvest this "destroyer of weakness." Each batch undergoes traditional Ayurvedic purification methods to preserve its raw potency, delivering the ultimate essence of the earth directly to you.
